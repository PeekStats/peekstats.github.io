Features

Know who you're playing against

🎯

Per-Map Stats

K/D, win rate and ADR for every player on all 8 active maps — Mirage, Inferno, Dust2, Nuke, Ancient, Overpass, Anubis, Cache.

📊

Team Averages

Side-by-side comparison of both teams across every map. See instantly which maps favor your side.

🗳️

Live Veto Tracker

Watch bans in real time with full team attribution — see exactly who banned what, and the final pick highlighted in green.

⚡

Auto-Accept

Optional toggle that auto-clicks the ready-check popup. Never miss a queue pop while you're afk.

🎨

Clean & Draggable

Move the overlay anywhere, collapse to a corner icon when you don't need it. Dark theme that matches FACEIT.

🔒

Zero Tracking

No accounts, no analytics, no ads. Stats fetched directly from FACEIT's official API. Code is open and reviewable.
